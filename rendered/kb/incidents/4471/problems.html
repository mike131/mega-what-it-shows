Each problem has its own ticket in mike131/mega-what-it-shows:

  1. The parser (#1): the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant books 7 April, and the invite says 4 July. The fix that #4471 queued is for this problem.
  2. The booking-v3 hint (#2): the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it tells them, and the form reads their date the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not reach it. Only reports sent through that form belong here.
  3. The one-day moves (#3): the date moves by one day in the same month, and two people in different time zones get different days for one meeting. No month and day are swapped. The desk listed these reports in desk-notes.json; the #4471 table counts them under the parser, by tenant.

How the reports got merged

Decision 0001 merges reports on subject alone, and nobody reads the body. The same subject lines cover all three problems, so the rule put them in one ticket. The cause comes from the tenant’s locale, its booking form and the channel (causes.ts), and from what the body says. Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser: do not count by it.

For the desk

  • Do not close #4471 when the parser fix ships. Check the hint and one-day tickets first.
  • When the reporter’s tenant runs booking-v3 and they came in through the form, file the report under the hint ticket.
  • When the body names the same month and a day one off, file it under the one-day ticket and add its id to desk-notes.json.
